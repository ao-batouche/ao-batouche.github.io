I had the great pleasure of speaking at <a href="https://www.bioinf.fi/?page_id=285" target="_blank"> Bioinformatics Day 2026</a>. In my talk, I presented our recent work on using machine learning to develop multimodal classification models trained on a multi-institutional cohort to predict adverse pathology (AP) in prostate cancer.

Many thanks to my supervisors and collaborators for their support and contributions. Thank you also to <a href="https://www.bioinf.fi/" target="_blank"> The Finnish Society for Bioinformatics </a> for organising such a great event.
