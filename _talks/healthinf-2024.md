I presented our paper at **HEALTHINF 2024**, the 17th International Conference on Health Informatics, in Rome, Italy.

The work addresses a practical challenge in clinical research: electronic health records contain valuable information, but it is often split between incomplete structured fields and unstructured clinical notes. We developed and validated a pipeline that uses the overlap between these sources to extract prostate cancer data and build more complete, reliable research datasets.

The paper, *Synergizing Data Imputation and Electronic Health Records for Advancing Prostate Cancer Research: Challenges, and Practical Applications*, was published in the **BIOSTEC 2024 — HEALTHINF proceedings**, pages 77–86. The publication and open preprint are linked below.
