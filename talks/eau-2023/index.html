EAU 2023 · Conference presentation

Prognostic impact of prostate cancer grade inflation in targeted biopsies

10–13 March 2023 · Milan, Italy

I presented our work on prostate cancer grade inflation in targeted biopsies at the 38th Annual Congress of the European Association of Urology (EAU23) in Milan, Italy. The congress took place on 10–13 March 2023.

The presentation examined the prognostic meaning of grade inflation in the context of targeted prostate biopsies. This research asks how changes in diagnostic methods affect the interpretation of tumour grade and the assessment of prostate cancer risk.

The associated meeting abstract, Prognostic impact of prostate cancer grade inflation in targeted biopsies, was published in European Urology. The full citation and DOI are available below.
