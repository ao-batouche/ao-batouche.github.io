Use case: scientific-educational, conceptual editorial illustration (not a data visualization).
Asset type: high-resolution landscape 16:10 cover for an academic portfolio project named PCC-RAG, a retrieval-augmented assistant for prostate cancer patients that grounds its answers in source documents.
Primary request: Create a sophisticated, quiet editorial illustration about retrieving reliable clinical evidence and citing it in a helpful answer. A few elegantly layered ivory paper evidence cards, each with small abstract typographic lines and a distinct source-tab, connect through a small restrained retrieval lens to one clear response card with unobtrusive citation markers. The forms should feel intentional, tactile, and beautifully composed, like a serious scientific journal's commissioned cover, with subtle paper grain and very soft shadows. Warm off-white field, deep forest green and muted sage with small soft gold details. Large simple composition legible as a website thumbnail; generous breathing room, balanced across the full frame, crisp precise edges. No text labels or title; abstract lines only, no readable prose. No charts or fabricated numerical results. No humans, robot heads, chatbot faces, human organs, medical anatomy, futuristic holograms, glowing brains, neon, circuits, screenshot UI chrome, generic network webs, 3D stock-art look, gradients, logos, watermark, or border. This must be a professional concept illustration, with no implication that it shows an actual product interface. Output one finished landscape image.
