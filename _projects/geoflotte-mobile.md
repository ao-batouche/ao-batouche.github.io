**Geoflotte i2b** is a mobile fleet management application published by [i2b SPA](https://i2b-dz.com/). It gives businesses access to information about their vehicles, machinery, and mobile equipment from an iPhone or iPad.

[View on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/geoflotte-i2b/id6791266452)

## Features

- **Live fleet map:** locate vehicles and check their current position and speed.
- **Fleet overview:** monitor vehicle activity from a dashboard and searchable vehicle list.
- **Trip history:** review journeys, movements, and stops.
- **Vehicle alerts:** follow alerts associated with the fleet.

Access requires an active professional account provided by Geoflotte. Available features depend on the company's contract, installed equipment, and user permissions.
