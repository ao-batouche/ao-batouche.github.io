I presented our research on **joint modelling of time to curative treatment and prostate cancer recurrence** at the 9th Baltic Meeting in conjunction with the European Association of Urology, held on 24–25 May 2024 in Tallinn, Estonia.

The talk was followed by a poster presentation, and our work received the **second-best-presentation award**. The research explores the relationship between treatment timing and subsequent recurrence through a joint-modelling approach.

Two related abstracts were accepted at the meeting and published in **European Urology Open Science**: the joint-modelling study presented in this talk, and a companion study on preliminary evidence of Gleason grade inflation before treatment. Both are linked below.
