What an incredible week in New York City at the first edition of VibeCon by <a href='https://www.replit.com'  target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" >Replit</a>.

A real highlight: getting to meet one of the leading voices in AI and vibe coding, **Amjad Masad**.
The talks, panel discussions, and workshops were genuinely thought-provoking, exploring the future (or should I say the present!) of AI-powered coding.
Grateful to have been part of this first edition.
