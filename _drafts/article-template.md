Open with the central idea in two or three sentences. Tell the reader why it matters.

## The main point

Explain one idea at a time. Short paragraphs make the article easy to read on the website and easy to adapt into a LinkedIn post.

Use a small list when it helps:

- First practical point.
- Second practical point.
- Third practical point.

## What I learned

Add the observation, result, or lesson you want the reader to remember.

## Takeaway

Finish with a concise conclusion or a question that invites discussion.
