Talk: Multi-model Machine learning models from cross-institutional cohorts

May 29, 2026

2026

I had the great pleasure of speaking at Bioinformatics Day 2026. In my talk, I presented our recent work on using machine learning to develop multimodal classification models trained on a multi-institutional cohort to predict adverse pathology (AP) in prostate cancer.

Many thanks to my supervisors and collaborators for their support and contributions. Thank you also to The Finnish Society for Bioinformatics for organising such a great event.
